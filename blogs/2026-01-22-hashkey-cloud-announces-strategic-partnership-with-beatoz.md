HashKey Cloud Announces Strategic Partnership with BEATOZ to Strengthen its Web3 Infrastructure by joining as a Network Validator HONG KONG — January 16, 2026 — HashKey Cloud, a leading global Web3 infrastructure provider, today announced the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with blockchain enterprise BEATOZ. Under the agreement, HashKey Cloud will officially join the BEATOZ mainnet as a network validator supporting the network’s core security and consensus mechanism. BEATOZ is an EVM-compatible mainnet dedicated to the tokenization of financial assets.
