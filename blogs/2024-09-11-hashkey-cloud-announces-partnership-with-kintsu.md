[Sept 11, 2024, HKSAR] HashKey Cloud is excited to announce our partnership with Kintsu , a pioneering next-generation liquid node validation infrastructure built on the Monad Network. This collaboration aims to enhance the performance and security of Kintsu’s liquid node validation protocol and the Monad Network by leveraging HashKey Cloud’s robust and reliable infrastructure solutions. What Does Kintsu Do Kintsu is a liquid node validation protocol on the Monad Network that enhances the economic potential of Proof-of-Stake (PoS) blockchains.
