Exploring the Role of Babylon Babylon’s restaking works by locking BTC to the Bitcoin mainnet and then providing shared security for PoS consumer chains. Restaking means you can earn yields with your yield-bearing staked funds again. In EigenLayer, restaked ETH can earn both AVS and ETH2 PoS rewards (we don’t consider potential airdrops here).
